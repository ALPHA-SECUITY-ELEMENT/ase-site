WHO WE ARE

A TEAM,
NOT A CLAN.

Alpha Security Element is an Arma Reforger PvP-only unit. We exist on Old Guard Revival. We exist for organized combat. We exist for the players who want to fight as one.

01 / MISSION

BUILD A TEAM THAT FIGHTS.

Our mission is not to be the biggest. It is to be the most capable. A team that communicates, adapts, and wins because every member knows their job and trusts the person next to them.

No scripted PvE. No unnecessary bureaucracy. No elitist attitude. Just Arma Reforger PvP, teamwork, and a community that enjoys playing together.

02 / PRINCIPLES

WHAT WE STAND ON.

  • Communicate. Tell your team what you're doing.
  • Listen. Communication goes both ways.
  • Adapt. PvP rarely goes exactly to plan.
  • Support. Help teammates instead of chasing kills.
  • Improve. Everyone starts somewhere.
  • Have fun. At the end of the day, it's a game.
03 / DOCTRINE

HOW WE OPERATE.

01

BRIEF

Every operation starts with a clear brief. Objectives, roles, comms channels, contingencies.

02

DEPLOY

Fireteams move as elements, not individuals. Leaders call, operators execute.

03

ADAPT

Contact changes everything. Comms relay, elements shift, the plan flexes without collapsing.

04

DEBRIEF

After action, honest review. What worked, what didn't, what we drill next.
