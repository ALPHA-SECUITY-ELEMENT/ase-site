Your First 5 Minutes
Follow these steps to complete your first investigation.
1
Open a Nightjar server
Click NIGHTJAR in the sidebar. You'll see the Server Hub. Start with SCRIPT KIDDIES — the easiest server to learn on.
2
Watch the chat scroll in
Messages arrive automatically. Suspects appear in the left sidebar as they post. Watch who gives orders and who takes them.
3
Click the highlighted clues
Words like 45.133.1.88 are clickable. Each click logs an evidence item in the right panel.
4
Switch channels
Click any channel in the left sidebar or type switch #drops in the command bar.
5
File a KODEX request
Click New Request in the sidebar. Choose Discord + Legal Process, enter a suspect's handle as the subject, submit. It goes to Pending Review — meet the Nightjar requirements to trigger a provider response.
6
Meet the requirements
Open the request detail (Dashboard → click request). You'll see "Nightjar Requirements." For Legal Process, log at least 1 evidence item against that suspect. Then the provider responds.
7
Arrest the ringleader
Once unmasked, right-click them in Nightjar or type arrest <handle>. If you got the right person — the ringleader — the server is cleared.
8
Check the rest of the portal
Click Documents for case files, Messages for DM intercepts, and Agency Profile to change your codename and affiliation.

Every server follows the same completion path.

◉ The 6-Step Completion Checklist
  1. Let the chat finish playing. Don't exit until all channels have stopped producing messages.
  2. Log every clue. Click every highlighted term, in every channel.
  3. Log every audio attachment. Click ♪ cards → Play → Log as Evidence.
  4. File KODEX requests. New Request → Discord → Legal Process. Subject = suspect handle.
  5. Unmask the ringleader. Once their request completes, their IP shows in green.
  6. Arrest the ringleader. Right-click → Submit arrest warrant, or arrest <handle>.
◉ What Fails a Case
  • Arresting the wrong suspect. Ringleader escapes; case fails.
  • Timer hits zero. Suspects scatter before you serve the warrant.
  • A counter-trace hits 100%. Suspects sever your connection (see the Counter-Trace tab).

⚠
Counter-Trace & Takedowns
When the suspects realize they're being watched.
◉ How Counter-Traces Work
If you spend too long in a server (usually when the timer drops below 3 minutes), the suspects may detect your monitoring tools. A red COUNTER-TRACE bar will appear at the top of the chat feed.

You have roughly 5 seconds to sever the connection before the bar reaches 100%. If it hits 100%, the server deploys a kill-switch, you are kicked out, and the case fails immediately.
◉ How to Survive a Trace
To stop the trace, you must force the platform to kill the connection from their end. You do this by filing a Content Takedown request in KODEX.
  1. Immediately switch to the Dashboard.
  2. Click New Request.
  3. Select Discord and Content Takedown.
  4. Enter the handle of any suspect you've already seen in the server.
  5. Submit the request.
  6. Fulfill the requirement: Mark the suspect as priority in Nightjar (right-click them → ★ Set priority).
Once the provider processes the takedown, the server connection is severed safely, the trace meter turns green, and you can resume your investigation.

◉ Strategic Tips
  • Don't hint early. Costs 4,000–7,500 pts on hard servers.
  • Unmask before arrest. +300 pts per unmask.
  • Wiretap early is worth it. 500 pts for a suspect's next DM.
  • Burn selectively. 1,000 pts is steep — use on a likely ringleader.
  • Loudest is often a decoy. In hard servers, watch who defers.
◉ Why is my request stuck on "Pending Review"?
You haven't met all the Nightjar requirements shown in the request detail. Complete each requirement to trigger a provider response.
◉ Why does my IP show as [ENCRYPTED]?
Because that suspect hasn't been unmasked via KODEX yet. File a Discord Legal Process request for their handle.
◉ How do I know who the ringleader is?
Watch for authority cues: who sets rules, who takes the largest cut, who others defer to. Type hint for a description of the ringleader's pattern.
◉ The red trace bar is filling up! What do I do?
Go to the KODEX Dashboard immediately and file a Content Takedown request for the server. Mark the suspect as priority in Nightjar to fulfill the requirement. This severs the trace.
◉ What are Geofence and Pen Register requests?
Geofence: Requires you to unmask 2 suspects in the same server. Pen Register: Requires 3 evidence items total. Both are high-level warrants that yield massive intel bonuses.
◉ What happens if I arrest the wrong suspect?
The case fails. The real ringleader escapes. Retry from the hub, or move on.
